Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

The short version

This is a personal portfolio website run by Khushi Kakar. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising, and it does not ask for or collect personal information.

What is stored on your device

If you play the hidden circuit game, your best time is saved in your browser's local storage so it can be shown next time. It stays on your device and is never sent to me or anyone else. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Information handled by others

  • Google Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them, so Google receives your IP address and basic browser details under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Hosting. Whoever hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, date and time, and pages requested, for security and reliability. I do not use these logs to identify visitors.
  • Links. Links to LinkedIn, GitHub and email open other services that have their own privacy practices.

Email

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. I use it only to reply, I do not sell or share it, and I will delete our conversation if you ask.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California privacy law), you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because this site collects none itself, this mainly applies to emails we exchange. Write to me and I will respond.

Security

The site sets a strict content-security policy, loads no third-party scripts and contains no trackers.
